Sky Phoenix City

Fongshan Station is Kaohsiung’s first railway station to integrate parking facilities with a commercial complex.

The newly developed “Sky Phoenix City” draws inspiration from the historic Fongshan Old City, blending transportation, commerce, and cultural identity into a unified space.

Life in Motion

At Fongshan Station, architecture and transit evolve together. As the structure takes shape, every operation is precisely orchestrated to maintain uninterrupted rail service—transforming progress into a seamless continuation of daily life.
